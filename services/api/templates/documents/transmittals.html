{% translate "What a transmittal is" %}

{% blocktranslate %}The formal act of handing documents to someone outside. It says what was sent, which revision of each thing, to whom, when, and whether they acknowledged it.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %}It is the difference between «I sent it to you» and being able to prove it six months later. If the client says they never got the revision B of a drawing, this is what answers — with its date and its list.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %}It does not send the files: it records the delivery and notifies the recipients. What travels is the register, not a copy in somebody's inbox.{% endblocktranslate %} {% translate "More words like this one" %}
